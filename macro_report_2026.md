# Global Consumer Goods Market and Macroeconomic Dynamics
**Verification of 2025 Forecasts and Strategic Perspectives for 2026 to 2035**

***

An analysis of financial and consumer markets at the turn of 2025 and 2026, based on global data from Euromonitor International, the International Monetary Fund, and stock market reports, provides clear evidence of a profound structural transformation. This transformation has redefined the concept of luxury, technological efficiency, and the balance of economic power. The year 2025, described in forecasts as a time of supply shocks and the artificial intelligence revolution, fully realized the assumed scenarios, and in many aspects, such as the capitalization of the technology sector in the US or Poland's economic advancement in Central Europe, significantly exceeded them.

## Architecture of the Global Economy in 2025 to 2026
The global macroeconomic landscape in 2025 was shaped by two dominant phenomena. The unprecedented concentration of capital in the artificial intelligence sector and the return of aggressive protectionism in United States trade policy. These factors created a unique operating environment for European and Asian FMCG and luxury goods companies, forcing margin and logistical flexibility.

### The Nvidia Phenomenon and the AI Era on Global Markets
In October 2025, Nvidia's market capitalization crossed the $5 trillion barrier on the American stock exchange, marking a staggering 44% year over year growth. Nvidia controlled over 80% of the global market for chips used in AI data centers and held a 92% share in the discrete GPU market. Its order book at the end of 2025 stood at half a trillion dollars, driven primarily by demand from American and Asian technology giants.

### Tariff Shock and Protectionism in the US
Donald Trump's trade policy shook markets on April 1, 2025, with the announcement of radical import tariffs imposed on goods from Europe and Asia. US budget revenues from tariffs quadrupled between June 2024 and November 2025. Swiss companies like Givaudan implemented price increases in 2025 to fully offset the negative effects of tariffs in the North American market and maintain global profit margins.

## Poland as the Economic Phenomenon of the CEE Region
Poland not only maintained its status as the fastest growing large economy in the European Union but also dominated the Central and Eastern Europe region, achieving symbolic milestones in global rankings.

✦ **Surpassing Japan** According to IMF forecasts, income per capita in Poland at purchasing power parity surpassed that of Japan. Poland became the 20th largest economy in the world.
✦ **Economic Growth** GDP growth in 2025 was 3.4%, outclassing the stagnating economies of Germany and France, with a forecast for Poland at 3.7% for 2026.
✦ **Stock Market Success** Orlen became the first Polish company to break the 100 billion PLN capitalization barrier on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, reaching 125.6 billion PLN in February 2026, followed by PKO BP at 111.9 billion PLN.
✦ **Demographic Challenges** Births in the country fell below 250,000 annually, while deaths exceeded 400,000, which rapidly accelerates the need for the automation of domestic industry.

## Fragrance Sector 2025 ✦ Recession Glam in Western Markets
✦ **Sol de Janeiro** The brand maintained its status as the most popular brand in the Sephora network in North America and number one in the fragrance category on the US and UK Amazon. It controls a 32% share of the body spray category on US ecommerce platforms.
✦ **The Body Mist Boom** The body mist market in the United States alone reached a value of over $6 billion, with equally strong dynamics in Western Europe. Data confirms a strong high end mist niche, where price is no longer a barrier but an indicator of cultural aesthetics.
✦ **Scent Stacking** Global data from Pinterest for 2026 shows a 500% increase in searches for niche perfume collections and a 125% increase for perfume layering combinations.

## Industry Leaders ✦ The Expansion of Givaudan and L’Occitane
✦ **Givaudan** Average like for like sales growth in 2021 to 2025 was 6.8%, significantly exceeding the global 4 to 5% target. The Fine Fragrances division grew by 18.3% in 2025, driven by massive demand in the US and the Middle East. The 2030 strategy plans to invest approximately 8% of revenues annually in innovations, including AI platforms.
✦ **L’Occitane Group** Net sales reached €2.8 billion. The Sol de Janeiro brand now accounts for 31.6% of the group total sales, dominating the Americas and the dynamically growing APAC region.

## Forecasts for 2026 to 2035
✦ **Functional Fragrance** Consumer behavior research in developed markets indicates that a full 71% of global consumers expect fragrances to offer mood enhancing benefits. Leaders like Givaudan are investing in active neurological ingredients, like Cereboost.
✦ **Blue Beauty and Biotech** A shift in European and American laboratories towards lab produced plant molecules via fermentation, intended to decouple the industry from disruptions in agricultural supply chains.
✦ **Vamp Romantic and Glitchy Glam** Global Gen Z preferences in Western metropolises are driving dark, bold aesthetics alongside asymmetrical visual concepts.
✦ **The Russian Market** Driven by forced territorial isolation and 35% import tariffs on goods from Western countries, the domestic Russian market is forecast to grow from $672.5 million in 2024 to nearly $1.1 billion in 2035. Local producers now control 68% of the local market.

## Strategic Insights ✦ Consumer Psychology and Jobs to be Done
Based on the macroeconomic framework above, in 2026 the target customer is not buying perfume solely to smell pleasant. They are hiring the product to perform a specific emotional and psychological job.

✦ **Job 1 ✦ Anesthesia and Focus**
> *I hire a functional mist with active neurocosmetics to quickly and discreetly lower my stress levels in a high pressure environment, without having to take a physical break from work.*

✦ **Job 2 ✦ Protection of Unique Identity**
> *I hire the Scent Stacking ritual and combine several fragrances to create a completely unique scent bubble that no one in my environment will be able to copy or identify.*

✦ **Job 3 ✦ Flawless Investment Verification**
> *I hire a visit to a physical drugstore to ensure that the fragrance I fell in love with via an AI algorithm on my screen actually synergizes with my skin chemistry before I risk losing my capital.*

✦ **Job 4 ✦ Ethical Luxury without Compromise**
> *I hire niche perfumes created through the method of biotechnological fermentation to feel absolute sophistication and status, while reassuring my conscience that I am not contributing to the destruction of biodiversity.*
