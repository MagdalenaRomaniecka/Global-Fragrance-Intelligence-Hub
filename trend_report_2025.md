## Market Analysis and Consumer Trends

### The Sol de Janeiro Phenomenon
As FMCG sector analysts, we observe the unprecedented dominance of the Sol de Janeiro brand in the mass premium segment. This brand builds its position not only on volume but on unique cultural positioning, which allows it to maintain higher margins compared to traditional drugstore brands.

**Key Success Factors**
✦ **Unique Scent Profiles (Gourmand)** Flagship compositions, such as Cheirosa 62 and Cheirosa 71, are based on edible notes that build a strong emotional connection with the consumer.
✦ **Scent Stacking** The brand successfully implemented the strategy of layering products (body wash, cream, mist), drastically increasing the frequency of repurchases.
✦ **Recession Glam** In an era of economic uncertainty, consumers substitute high end luxury items with affordable, high quality sensory experiences.

## Givaudan Strategy and the AI Revolution

### The Shift to Functional Fragrance
Givaudan, a global leader in the creation of flavors and fragrances, is redefining the market by moving from traditional perfumery to functional fragrances.

✦ **Neurocosmetics** The introduction of ingredients like Cereboost, designed to actively lower cortisol levels and improve cognitive function through olfactory stimulation.
✦ **AI Integration** The use of platforms like Myrissi to predict emotional responses to scent profiles, allowing for hyper targeted product development based on real time data rather than pure intuition.

## Strategic Outlook for 2025
The fragrance market is no longer solely about aesthetics. It is fundamentally about emotional regulation. Brands that successfully merge accessible luxury with functional, neuro cosmetic benefits will capture the majority of the market share moving into the next fiscal year.
