✦ **Green Shift** 46 percent of EU consumers prioritize refillable or natural attributes driving the natural market to 60 billion USD.

## Russian Federation Market Autarky
✦ **Domestic Output** Record breaking 93 Million units produced in 2024.
✦ **Consumer Spending** 46.5 Billion Rubles spent in the first quarter of 2024 according to Chestny ZNAK tracking data. The strict duhi category generated 5.3 billion rubles.
✦ **Supply Chain Pivot** A 35 percent tariff on unfriendly nations boosted UAE imports by 139 percent and Turkish imports by 47 percent.
