### Part III. Market Trends ✦ Gourmand 2.0 to Moody Aquatics

**HOST**
So if Sol de Janeiro is the poster child for edible comfort, what else is happening in 2026?

**CO HOST**
The landscape is fragmented, but there are four main pillars.

1. **Gourmand 2.0** The evolution of the sweet trend. The first version was the Pink Sugar era, loud and synthetic. The 2.0 version is sophisticated. Creamy pistachio paired with dark woods, whipped cream cut with spicy cardamom. It is a warm hug, not a sugar rush.
2. **Skin Scents** The no makeup trend of perfume. Designed for open plan offices using large molecules like **Iso E Super** or **Ambroxan**. It smells like clean laundry or salty skin. Defensive perfumery.
3. **Moody Aquatics** Not the Cool Water of the 90s. These are earthy, naturalistic scents like algae, seaweed, iodine, and salt. The smell of the ocean on a stormy day.
4. **Botanical Greens** A rejection of the digital world. Photorealistic tomato leaf, crushed stems, and basil.

**HOST**
And the last pillar?

**CO HOST**
The death of the binary. The labels **Pour Homme** and **Pour Femme** look incredibly dated. Gen Z and Gen Alpha choose scent based on mood, not gender marketing.
